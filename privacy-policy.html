Privacy Policy - Worth My Time

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Worth My Time works without an account, developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, or application network requests. Information entered into the app is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export it.

Information stored on the device

The app may locally store profile nicknames, optional age ranges, income and allowance amounts, work schedules, purchases and notes, savings goals, household relationships, reflections, recurring plans, wait-and-revisit items, enjoyment-fund records, calculation snapshots, and app preferences. It does not ask for legal names, exact dates of birth, bank credentials, payment-card data, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.

The local store uses Apple's SwiftData framework, and the app requests complete file protection. Protection behavior ultimately depends on your device passcode and operating system.

Collection, tracking, and third parties

The app contains no application networking, third-party SDK, tracker, ad SDK, crash-reporting service, account service, cloud sync, or push notifications. The developer does not receive any data you enter into the app.

App lock and child profiles

The optional app lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication with device-owner authentication. Worth My Time never receives biometric data or your passcode. Child profiles use a nickname and an optional broad age range only; leaving child mode requires device-owner authentication. The feature is intended for use on a parent or guardian's device and does not create a child account.

Exports and backups

Exports are user-initiated and require device-owner authentication. PDF reports and CSV files are not encrypted after export; sensitive PDF fields are excluded by default. Encrypted .wtb backups use a password you choose; the password is not saved and cannot be recovered. The app never uploads exported files or backups.

Deletion

You can delete everything at any time with Delete All Data in Settings, which removes all stored records and returns the app to onboarding. Removing the app removes its local data container, subject to Apple's device backup behavior.

Changes

Any release that introduces data collection, network services, third-party SDKs, or cloud synchronization will update this policy and the App Store privacy answers before distribution.
