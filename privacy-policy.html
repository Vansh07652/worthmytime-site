Private by design

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Worth My Time is a local, offline-first calculator. It has no account system, developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, trackers, cloud storage, or third-party application dependencies. The developer does not receive the pay, price, item, settings, or calculation information you enter.

Information stored on your device

Your calculator settings and up to 10 recent calculations are stored in your browser’s local storage. This data remains in that browser profile on that device. Storage may be unavailable in some private-browsing or restricted modes; the calculator will still work for the current session.

Network and offline use

Your first visit loads the static app files from GitHub Pages. A service worker then stores those files locally so the calculator can work offline. The app does not send your calculator entries anywhere. GitHub may process standard web-hosting request information under its own privacy terms when you load the hosted site.

Sharing

The Share result button acts only when you choose it. It uses your device’s share sheet when available or copies result text to your clipboard. Worth My Time does not choose a recipient or upload the result.

Deletion

Use Clear history to delete saved calculations. Reset calculator restores default settings but intentionally keeps history. You can also remove all locally stored app data through your browser’s site-data controls.

Changes

If a future version introduces data collection, accounts, analytics, or cloud features, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
