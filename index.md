# Worth My Time - Support

Worth My Time is a private, local-only iPhone and iPad app that turns purchase prices into a personal work-time perspective. No account, no ads, no tracking - your information stays on your device.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add or edit a person's income?** Open the Household tab and tap a person to edit their income, schedule, allowance, and currency.

**Where is my data stored?** Everything you enter stays on your device. Use Settings to export a PDF report, a CSV file, or a password-encrypted backup.

**I forgot my backup password.** Backup passwords cannot be recovered. Your current in-app data is unaffected; create a new backup with a new password.

**How do I delete my data?** Settings, then Delete All Local Data, removes everything and returns the app to onboarding.
